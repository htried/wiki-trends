TrendingPage explorer
Associated pages: the page's own article links and the pages that send it readers (Wikipedia clickstream, monthly), ranked by their views this hour. "vs usual" compares a page's views this hour with its average over the previous 96 hours. The clickstream column compares each source page's share of the group's views now with its share of the page's incoming clicks last month. Geography is daily. Data: Wikimedia Enterprise pageviews API (beta).
